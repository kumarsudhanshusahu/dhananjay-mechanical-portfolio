01 // Background & Motivation

About Me

An engineering undergraduate passionate about translating theoretical mechanics, thermodynamics, and materials science into precision physical components and manufacturing workflows.

I am an aspiring mechanical engineer currently pursuing my B.Tech in Mechanical Engineering at Centurion University of Technology and Management. My academic journey is centered on understanding how mechanical systems are conceived, mathematically modeled, virtually simulated, and fabricated for real-world reliability.

Core Career Focus: Seeking internship and junior engineering opportunities in Mechanical Design, CAD/CAE Modeling, Production & Manufacturing Systems, and Automotive Engineering where I can contribute to design verification, drafting, and hands-on fabrication.

Rather than viewing engineering strictly as abstract formulas, I enjoy applying first-principles thinking—from analyzing stress concentrations and heat dissipation in machine elements to studying CNC toolpaths, GD&T standards, and tolerances for lean manufacturing.

I am committed to authentic, disciplined engineering development, sharpening my foundation in tools such as AutoCAD, SolidWorks, CATIA, and basic FEA structural analysis alongside practical workshop machining.
